How to read this comparison

Windows end at the labeled target and use only past and current predictions. Historical monitor verdicts are not human labels. These results measure classification at selected frames; they do not measure onset detection delay or prevented safety events. The reported intervals are exploratory and are not adjusted for multiple policy comparisons.
