Selection is complete; annotation comes next

The real export opens in the existing annotator with its verdicts unanswered. It retains the source frame, categories and selection reason.

  1. Judge CoT grounding, prescribed-action safety and coherence.
  2. Judge CoT–trajectory consistency and planned-trajectory safety.
  3. Export annotations for review before updating the benchmark.

The existing annotator shows trajectory evidence alongside its questions. If your protocol requires a blinded reliability pass, keep that pass restricted to the model’s available input evidence.

How this example differs from the real workspace

This walkthrough uses one invented clip, three decision timestamps and a small category table. The real workspace has actual rollout videos, extracted input frames, all CoT categories, adjustable targets, and model-suggestion imports.

Here, targets are 3 for speed adaptation and 10 for yielding. They illustrate how counts change; they are not recommended sample sizes. Selection is manual, and one chosen timestamp replaces any earlier choice from the same clip.

The practice stays in this page’s memory. It does not use the real selector’s autosave, call a model, or change your benchmark. Restarting or reloading resets it.
